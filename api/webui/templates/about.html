Your Canvas in Developer Mode

Canvas Expert turns the hours you spend wrestling Canvas forms into minutes. Describe what you want to your AI, get a clean file back, and push it to the courses you choose: quizzes, assignments, pages, late policies, extensions, curves, and more.

What it does

Work tools

One push, every course. A quiz with 12 question types, per-choice rationales so students can learn WHY they were wrong (or right), and 3 differentiated tiers (if you ask). Author once — Canvas Expert fans it out to every course you check, matching grading categories, modules, and groups by name, not by fragile IDs.

And when work comes back: download every submission, organized by assignment and by student, with a CSV index.

Gradebook tools

The end of late-work spreadsheet math. Set a course-wide late policy in one click. Grant extensions that actually move due dates. Curve with four models, preview-first. Every grade change is previewed before it happens and explained where it lands.

Scoring Sessions. Ask your connected AI to start one for every assignment currently needing scoring across your Current courses. It works through one pseudonymized assignment packet at a time and sends valid results to Canvas Live, where you can review or edit them. Canvas Expert does not host a grading model.

AI helper files

Bring your own AI. Whatever assistant your district gives you. Canvas Expert hands it paste-ready skill files that teach it to author Canvas content and use Scoring Sessions. Your AI reads the SAFE packet; Canvas Expert keeps identities and Canvas writes local.

The Forges — your AI's grammar

Every piece of content starts as a Forge file: a strict, documented JSON contract (<QUIZFORGE_JSON>, <ASSIGNMENTFORGE_JSON>, <PAGEFORGE_JSON>). You describe the quiz you want; your AI writes the file; Canvas Expert validates it and pushes it. No ambiguity, no lost details, no manual form-filling. Each push box has a built-in "Forge one with your LLM" helper so the right skill is always one click away.

Pedagogy is the default, not an option

  • Every answer choice gets a rationale. Students who miss a question see why their specific wrong answer was wrong — the misconception, named — not just the right answer.
  • Differentiation is one file, not three. Tiers ride along in the same assignment file and auto-assign to named Canvas groups. Same learning target, different scaffolds, invisible to students.
  • Answers shuffle, results show by design. Quizzes shuffle choices by default and reveal rationales after the last attempt — the settings research says to use, pre-set.

Built safe by design

Canvas Expert holds a key to your gradebook, so it's engineered like it matters:

When an assistant connects over the local MCP server, it receives pseudonymized reads. An explicit Scoring Session request authorizes that session's valid results to post to Canvas; identities, preflight and verification stay on this computer. Other bounded writes require their own reviewed operation.

  • Your token never touches a disk in plaintext. The Canvas access token lives in your operating system's credential store (Windows Credential Manager) — the same vault your OS uses for its own secrets. It is never written to a file, never synced to the cloud, and minted fresh on each machine you use.
  • Everything runs on your computer. The app serves itself at 127.0.0.1 — your own machine, full stop. There is no Canvas Expert server, no account to create, no third party between you and Canvas.
  • The AI never sees your token or a real student identity. When your assistant reads your class data through Canvas Expert, it comes pseudonymized: stable fake names stand in for real students, and Canvas user IDs and SIS IDs never leave this computer. Canvas Expert, local code you can read, is the only thing that talks to Canvas.
  • Credential-free by rule. The project rule is absolute — any tool that needs a credential is local-only, forever.
  • Grade changes are preview-first and transparent. Curves show you exactly what will change before anything is written, and changes are explained where students and parents can see them.
  • Defense in depth, even in development. The codebase is private, and an automated pre-commit guard blocks the Canvas token pattern from ever entering version control.

Where your content lives — your OneDrive

Canvas Expert keeps a single home for everything you author in a CanvasExpert folder inside your OneDrive — so your content syncs across every machine you sign in on, and stays inside your school's tenant. (No OneDrive? It falls back to local folders on that PC.) Here's what shows up in there:

  • Library — your authored Forge files (Quizzes, Assignments, Pages), plus Calendars, Source Materials, and the AI Authoring skill files Canvas Expert hands to your LLM. These are the folders the matching work-tool tabs read from, so a file you save here is ready to push from any machine.
  • To Review — assistant-staged drafts waiting for you to push to Canvas.
  • Printables — printable copies. Tick "Also save a printable version" when you push a quiz and the QuizForge DOCX bundle (student copy, answer key, rationale sheet) lands here automatically — ready to print, no extra steps.
  • Canvas Uploads — QTI/.imscc import packages.
  • Student Work — real-name student data: downloaded submissions, the per-student reports you generate, and grading-key crosswalks. This holds sensitive student data, so it lives here on purpose: inside your in-tenant OneDrive, FERPA-conscious, never sent anywhere else.
  • For AI — the pseudonymized packets you review before handing to an external AI. No real names.
  • Settings and support diagnostics — machine-local configuration, sanitized operational records, and support bundles you choose to export.

Two things deliberately never sync to OneDrive: your Canvas token (it stays in the OS credential store on each machine) and your monitored-students list and private notes (machine-local only). Authoring travels with you; secrets and watchlists stay put.

The whole workflow

  1. Describe what you want to your AI (with the Forge skill loaded).
  2. Paste or upload its file into the matching tool tab.
  3. Validate — see exactly what will push, catch errors early.
  4. Check your courses in the picker. One, some, or all.
  5. Push. Watch the per-course log. Done.

Get started

Add your Canvas token in Settings (30 seconds, one time), then head to the Work tools and push your first quiz. Start simple: one quiz, one course. You'll never fill in a Canvas form the same way again.
