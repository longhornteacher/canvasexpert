Connect your Canvas account

Create a personal access token in Canvas and paste it below. This token gives Canvas Expert access to your courses — it's stored in your Windows Credential Manager, never in any file.

How to get a token:
  1. Open your Canvas Settings ↗ — click your profile photo in the top-left of Canvas, then choose Settings
  2. Scroll down to Approved Integrations → click + New Access Token
  3. Name it Canvas Expert, leave expiry blank (unless your school requires one)
  4. Click Generate Token, then copy it
  5. Come back here and paste it below
